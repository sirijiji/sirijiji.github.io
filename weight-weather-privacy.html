Weight Weather — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Weight Weather ("the app") is a weight-tracking application. This policy explains what data the app handles. Short version: all data stays on your device. The app collects, transmits, and shares nothing.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter (your weight entries, profile details, and settings) locally in a database on your phone. Nothing you enter is transmitted to us or to any third party.

2. No data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, analytics, advertising identifiers, or device information. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

3. Notifications

The app can schedule daily reminder notifications. These are generated and delivered entirely on your device and can be disabled at any time from the app's settings or your system settings.

4. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions. It may request notification permission (Android 13+) solely to show the reminder you choose to enable.

5. Data deletion

You can delete any or all of your data at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Play Store page.
